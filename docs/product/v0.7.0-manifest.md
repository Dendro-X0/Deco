## Problem

- **Scan strategy** presets tune depth and concurrency only; they do not bundle scope, safety profile, or global-cache discovery for “first audit” vs “monorepo maintainer” vs “CI agent”.
- Global-cache candidates already carry regeneration text in the engine/CLI summary, but the desktop UI did not surface it as a dedicated, scannable block.

## Cleanup profile bundles

Implemented in `apps/frontend/src/lib/cleanup-profiles.ts` (persisted as `cleanup_profile` in settings JSON).

| Preset | `scan_scope` | Safety | `stale_days` | Scan strategy | Notable discovery |
|--------|--------------|--------|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **first_scan** | `all` | `safe` | 45 | Thorough | All global caches off; smart discovery off |
| **monorepo_maintainer** | `all` | `balanced` | 30 | Balanced | npm/pnpm/yarn/cargo/pip/uv caches on; smart discovery on |
| **ci_agent** | `drives` | `balanced` | 14 | Fast | npm/pnpm/cargo/go + ccache/sccache; no profile dev folders |

Manual edits to any bundled field switch the label to **Custom** (same pattern as scan strategy).
