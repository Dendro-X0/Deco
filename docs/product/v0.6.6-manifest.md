**Status:** Shipped (`v0.6.6`)

Parent: [v0.6.x-roadmap.md](v0.6.x-roadmap.md) · Builds on `v0.6.5` fast delete, coalescing, and accurate scan progress.

## Problem

Full-drive scans can surface **thousands** of `node_modules` rows. The UI, size phase, and delete pipeline still behave like “hundreds of items” workloads. On HDDs, parallel deletes and per-tree walks degrade non-linearly. Users need clearer grouping, honest progress, and cleanup strategies tuned for mechanical disks.
