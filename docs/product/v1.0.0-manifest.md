## Positioning

**Deco 1.0** is the **developer cleanup** release:

- Whitelist artifact kinds, explain every row, quarantine-first defaults.
- CLI and desktop share a **stable scan contract** and fixture-tested classification.
- **Tool storage migration** ships as a **Windows-only** Settings feature with clear labeling — not part of scan `schema_version`.
